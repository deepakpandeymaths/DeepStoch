WHAT DEEPSTOCH DOES

Forecast the future, measure uncertainty, and test whether the uncertainty is trustworthy.

DeepStoch is research software for time-series forecasting. It compares simple baselines, machine-learning models, neural networks, and stochastic state models. Its important output is not only a predicted value, but also a prediction interval and evidence showing whether that interval works on future data.
